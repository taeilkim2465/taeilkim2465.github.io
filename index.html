I am an undergraduate student in Electrical Engineering at KAIST and a researcher at the Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence Lab, advised by Prof. Sung Ju Hwang.

My research focuses on LLM agents, agent memory, and knowledge distillation. I study how agents can reuse successful experiences, transfer knowledge across tasks and models, and become more capable with limited resources.
